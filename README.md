# Pathwise

**Know the route. Not just the neighborhood.**

Pathwise is a location and route-intelligence app. Instead of stopping at a
neighborhood crime score or a turn-by-turn direction, Pathwise explains what
you'd actually encounter on a specific journey — walking, transit, or drive —
at a specific time of day, and why. It turns scattered public data (crime and
incident reports, pedestrian infrastructure, transit characteristics, activity
levels, and more) into a plain-language, explainable picture of a route or a
place, so people can make more informed decisions about how and where they
move, live, stay, and commute.

Pathwise isn't a "safety score" product. It's a route- and place-intelligence
layer that sits on top of maps, built around one core question: **what should
I know about this place, or the journey to and from it?**
